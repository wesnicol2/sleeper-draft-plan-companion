- Draft companion
    - Will keep up with draft and suggest players at each point based on draft plan (draft plan should be configured in config files only)
    - Auto update with top suggestions for each round depending on team construction + my preferences
    
            

Minimum viable product: A auto updating UI (no interaction needed from user) which shows top picks based on draft plan. Players should be highlighted with different colors based on how many draft plan criteria they have

NEXT STEPS: 
- Simplify my choices by highlighting the following:
    - synergy with existing team
    - offensive team rank (expected points to be scored)
    - strong handcuffs, 
    - bye week protection (negatively highlight players who have matching bye weeks to your existing players at that same position)
    - players with new, better coaches, 
    - players with new, better teammates, 
    - starter injury possibility, 
    etc...

- Explicitly identify dropoffs/deadzones for each position - for WR it's after Jaylen Cocher


- Player notes / snippets
    - When I hear something about a player (podcast, beat writer, injury chatter), log it against that player
    - A player with notes shows an icon on their card so it is obvious at a glance which players I have intel on
    - Clicking the icon opens that player's notes
    - Notes persist across sessions and are mine, not shared
    - Open questions to settle before building:
        - Are notes free text only, or also tagged (injury / role / coaching / sleeper)?
        - Can a note be added mid-draft, or is logging a pre-draft activity only?
          (the app is otherwise strictly no-interaction during a draft)
        - Do notes affect ranking or highlighting at all, or are they purely informational?


STRETCH GOALS: 
    - Show season long curve comparison between top 5 players or so based on season long props from odds api (stretch goal)
    - calculate predicted Wins Above Replacement (WAR) for every player and rank based on that
